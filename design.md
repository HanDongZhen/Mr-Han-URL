# Design direction

## Thesis
A quiet optical object in a dark studio. The first screen pairs oversized editorial typography with original refractive glass. Silver, graphite and a small amount of ice blue carry the entire site.

## Narrative
Opening light / personal identity → curiosity → fields of exploration → a full Velocity product scene → five related studies → hands-on HAN LAB → working philosophy → Keep building.

## Material
Layered highlights, thin cool borders, depth shadows, filtered backdrops and reflected light. The lazy WebGL overlay changes gently with pointer position. The hero image remains the reliable base on every device.

## Motion
Opening opacity, gentle scroll displacement, once-only section reveals, a QR scan beam, breathing assistant core and adjustable orbital movement. Motion can be paused. OS reduced-motion overrides all transitions. The WebGL renderer pauses when hidden/offscreen, caps rendering to ~30 fps and DPR to 1.25, and is skipped on narrow/low-core devices.

## Type and layout
System sans-serif, including Microsoft YaHei for Chinese. Oversized titles with restrained letter spacing; compact technical captions; a 1400px maximum content width. Mobile turns the hero into a vertical composition and product showcases into single-column chapters.

## Truthful content
All copy lives in `src/content.ts`. Keep Chinese and English entries paired. Use concepts and experiments to communicate intent without invented employment, awards, downloads or customer claims. GitHub is linked to the authenticated public profile. Public email remains opt-in.

## Interaction
Tabs support arrows/Home/End. Native dialogs provide modal focus containment, Escape dismissal and focus restoration. Every visual project has a text description. All motion studies are illustrative and say so. No placeholder links or fake contact submissions.
